Nudis Near Me v1.3

What it does

It asks iNaturalist which nudibranchs have been recorded within 50 km of you in the last 20 years, then sorts them by how often they turn up — so the top of the list is your best bet. Choosing rockpooling drops the species that only live below the tide line; choosing diving keeps everything, because anything findable in a rock pool is findable on a dive too.

Only Research Grade observations count unless you tick the "Needs ID" option, and there is a second pass you can switch on that adds museum and survey specimens from GBIF.

Where the shallow/deep split comes from

Neither iNaturalist nor GBIF records a usable depth for these animals, and WoRMS has no depth attributes for them either. So the split is a hand-maintained table in depths.js, with a genus-level fallback. Species with no entry are shown in both lists and badged depth unknown rather than guessed at.

No text, figures, or images have been taken from Willan & Davey's Super Sea Slugs (NIWA, 2020), which is licensed CC BY-NC-ND.

Photos

Photographs come from iNaturalist and are shown only where the photographer has applied a Creative Commons licence. Each one is credited. All-rights-reserved images are skipped, which is why a few species show a blank thumbnail.

Your location

Your position never leaves your device un-rounded — it is snapped to roughly 100 m before any search is sent, which makes no difference across a 50 km radius. Nothing is stored, and there is no analytics on this page.
