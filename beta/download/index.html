For large datasets

Occurd's live search fetches records instantly but caps at 10,000. Occurd Big Data uses GBIF's async download API — there's no cap, but GBIF prepares your file in the background (usually 1–5 minutes, sometimes longer for very large areas).

Results download as a CSV you can open in Excel, R, or anywhere else.

Requires a free GBIF account. GBIF tracks downloads for dataset citation and usage reporting — every download is attributed to a user. It's free and takes under a minute to set up.
